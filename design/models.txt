ENUMS
-----
MemberStatus: ACTIVE, SUSPENDED
StaffRole: EMPLOYEE, ADMIN
CopyStatus: AVAILABLE, ON_LOAN, RESERVED, LOST, DAMAGED   (RESERVED = held for a pickup or reservation)
CopyCondition: GOOD, WORN, DAMAGED
LoanStatus: REQUESTED, ACTIVE, RETURNED, OVERDUE, LOST, CANCELLED, EXPIRED
ReservationStatus: PENDING, READY, FULFILLED, EXPIRED, CANCELLED
FineReason: OVERDUE, DAMAGED, LOST
FineStatus: UNPAID, PARTIALLY_PAID, PAID, WAIVED


BUSINESS RULES
-----------------------------
- Students log in, see availability, reserve, request loans and pay fines.
- Borrowing: student requests a loan, staff confirm at pickup (REQUESTED -> ACTIVE).
- Returns are processed by staff.
- Unpaid fines (UNPAID or PARTIALLY_PAID) block new loan requests.
- Fine payment is simulated: student clicks "pay", the system records a Payment.
- Single library, H2 database.
