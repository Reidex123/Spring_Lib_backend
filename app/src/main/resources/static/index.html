How borrowing works

Four simple steps from search to shelf.

  1. 1

    Log in

    Sign in with your student account.

  2. 2

    Request

    Find a book and request an available copy.

  3. 3

    Collect

    Pick it up at the desk. Staff confirm your loan.

  4. 4

    Return

    Hand it back to staff and pay any fine online.

Unpaid fines pause new loan requests until they are paid.
